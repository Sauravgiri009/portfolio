# 99Shorts project rules (user overrides)

- Every on-screen text must be fully visible and readable: nothing cut off, nothing covered by badges or other elements, and no tiny text (body copy at least ~24 px on the 1080x1920 canvas).
- Title Case everywhere: first letter of every word capital, rest small (e.g. "Near Structural Completion", "Sq Ft"). Do not use CSS uppercase / all-caps labels. Acronyms such as BHK and NMS stay as they are.
- Area unit is written "Sq Ft".
- Prices always use the rupee symbol (₹), e.g. "₹1.30–1.74 Crore".
- Light / non-dark backgrounds, accent colour #FFD47B.
